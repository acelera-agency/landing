Frequently asked questions

Before we start.

Some answers to help you understand how we work.

contacto@acelera.agency ↗

How does the work begin?

With a 30-minute meeting to understand the process, the problem and the expected outcome. We then propose a concrete next step or explain why building is not the right move yet.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. The first stage usually takes one week, and a first version takes between two and six weeks. Before starting, we agree on stages, deliverables and dates.

Who owns the code?

The company that hires us. The code, repositories, documentation and access are in its name from the start.

Do all projects need AI?

No. We first understand the problem and choose a solution that makes sense. We add AI when it contributes to the outcome.

What happens after launch?

Your team can take over the system, or we can continue with maintenance, support and new versions. We agree on the arrangement before delivery.

Acelera uses the minimum information necessary to answer inquiries and assess possible projects. It does not sell data, has no advertising pixels of its own and does not use forms to subscribe people to marketing campaigns.

01

02

Scope

This policy applies to the data of people who:

  • visit Acelera's public website;
  • submit the contact form or popup form;
  • communicate through email or profiles linked from the site; or
  • hold commercial or precontractual conversations resulting from those contacts.

It does not govern data Acelera may process within a project commissioned by a client. Such processing must be defined in the proposal, contract and, where appropriate, a specific data processing agreement.

03

Data we process

Data you provide

  • Identification and professional contact information: first name, last name if provided, work email address and company.
  • Inquiry content: the process, problem, idea or context you choose to describe.
  • Subsequent communications: messages, contact details and documents exchanged to respond or evaluate a proposal.
  • Consent record: the required acceptance of this policy when submitting the form.

Technical and source data

  • Campaign source: the parameter utm_source, only when it exists in the address from which you submit the form.
  • Source page: the landing page's domain and path, without copying other URL parameters or the fragment.
  • Infrastructure data: IP address, date and time, browser, device, headers and security events recorded by hosting or distribution networks to deliver and protect the site.
  • Language preference: Spanish or English, stored locally in your browser.
  • Optional analytics: when you accept analytics, we record visited pages, traffic source, device, browser, approximate country or region, scroll depth, viewed sections, calls to action clicked, form start and outcome, and technical errors. We do not send form names, email addresses, companies or messages to PostHog.
  • Optional session recording: PostHog may visually reconstruct navigation to detect friction. All input fields are masked before leaving the browser, and form interactions are not autocaptured.

Do not send sensitive data, passwords, credentials, trade secrets or third-party personal data through the form. If you need to share confidential information to assess a project, we will first agree on a suitable channel and conditions.

04

How we use data

We use data only to:

  • receive, organize and answer your inquiry;
  • understand the problem, assess whether Acelera can help and prepare a next step or proposal;
  • maintain communications related to that request or a professional relationship;
  • identify, to a limited extent, the source that led to an inquiry;
  • measure site traffic, paths and conversions, improve the experience and detect usability problems, only when you accept analytics;
  • operate, maintain and protect the site, prevent abuse and resolve errors; and
  • comply with legal obligations, respond to valid requests and exercise or defend rights.

We do not sell or rent personal data. We also do not make automated decisions with legal or similar effects on people who contact Acelera.

The form does not include consent for newsletters or advertising. If a commercial subscription is offered in the future, it will be requested separately and will be optional.

05

Legal basis, consent and fields

Processing is carried out in accordance with Law 25,326, its Implementing Decree 1558/2001 and supplementary regulations.

By checking the box and submitting the form, you give free, express and informed consent for Acelera to process data for the purposes of this policy. We may also process information necessary to take precontractual steps you request, fulfill a professional relationship or comply with a legal obligation.

Name, work email address, company and acceptance of the privacy policy are required to process the form. Last name and process description are optional. Without the required fields, we cannot receive or answer a request through that channel.

You may withdraw consent for future processing by contacting the channel listed in section 1. Withdrawal does not affect previous lawful processing or data we must retain for a legal obligation or to manage an ongoing relationship.

06

Providers and recipients

Only team members who need to handle the inquiry and providers essential to operating the site and delivering communications have access to the data.

Vercel

Hosts the site and runs the function receiving the form. It may process technical data and request content during transmission. Privacy.

Resend · Plus Five Five, Inc.

Delivers the inquiry to Acelera by email. It processes sender, recipient, metadata and message content. Privacy.

Email provider

Acelera's operational inbox receives and retains the message so the team can respond. Access is restricted to authorized accounts.

Google Fonts

Delivers the site's fonts. It receives the browser's technical request; Google states that the API does not set cookies. Details.

cdnjs and jsDelivr

Distribute visual libraries used by the homepage. Like any distribution network, they receive technical data necessary to respond to the request.

PostHog

Provides product and web analytics, funnels, error detection and session recordings when you choose to accept. It processes technical and navigation events in its US Cloud region; form fields are masked and not included as event properties. The IP address may be used temporarily for approximate geolocation and automated traffic detection, then discarded under the project's configuration. Privacy.

Authorities and advisers

We may disclose information when required by law or a valid order, or when necessary to bring, exercise or defend claims.

LinkedIn is an external link: it receives information only when you choose to open it or communicate through that channel, under its own policies.

07

International transfers

Vercel, Resend, PostHog, Google and the distribution networks mentioned are global providers and may process data in the United States and other countries where they or their subprocessors operate. The United States is not currently on Argentina's list of jurisdictions with an adequate level of protection.

Depending on the service and data, these transfers rely on the data subject's informed consent, processing agreements incorporated into the services and available contractual safeguards. Acelera reviews whether the provider's use is compatible with the stated purpose and limits transmitted data to what is necessary.

The AAIP explains adequate countries and permitted mechanisms in its guide to international transfers.

08

Retention

  • Inquiries that do not become a contractual relationship: up to 24 months from the last interaction, unless deletion is requested earlier or there is a justified need to retain evidence of the communication.
  • Professional or contractual relationships: for the duration of the relationship and afterward for the periods necessary for legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to exercise or defend rights.
  • Language preference: remains in the browser's local storage until you delete it through browser controls.
  • Analytics choice: remains in the browser's local storage until you change it using “Analytics” in the site footer or delete the site's data.
  • Events and recordings: are retained for the periods configured in PostHog and deleted or anonymized when no longer necessary for the stated purposes.
  • Technical records: for the security and retention periods configured by infrastructure providers.

At the end of the applicable period, data is deleted, anonymized or blocked where an obligation prevents immediate deletion.

09

Your rights

You may request:

  • information and access to the data we retain;
  • updates or correction of inaccurate information;
  • deletion where applicable;
  • withdrawal of consent or objection to non-mandatory uses; and
  • information about recipients and transfers.

To exercise these rights, write to contacto@acelera.agency with the subject “Personal data request”. We may request the minimum information necessary to verify your identity and avoid disclosing data to another person.

The right of access is generally free of charge at intervals of at least six months, unless there is a legitimate interest, and must be answered within ten calendar days. Correction, updating or deletion must be handled within five business days, in accordance with the applicable statutory deadlines.

The Public Information Access Agency, as the supervisory authority under Law 25,326, is authorized to handle complaints and claims from people whose rights are affected by non-compliance with personal data protection rules. You may consult its channels and request templates.

10

Cookies and similar technologies

Acelera does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or advertising pixels. It uses PostHog for web and product analytics and session recordings only after you choose “Accept analytics”. If you choose “Essential only”, PostHog is not initialized.

The language selector stores in localStorage the key acelera-language, with the value “es” or “en”. It is a functional preference, is not used to identify you and is not sent with the form. You can delete it by clearing the site's data in your browser.

The choice is stored in localStorage under the key acelera-analytics-consent. If you accept, PostHog may use cookies or local storage to maintain an anonymous session across pages. You can change the choice at any time through the “Analytics” link in the footer; rejecting it stops future capture.

Google Fonts and distribution networks receive technical requests to deliver resources, as described in section 6.

11

Security and confidentiality

Acelera applies reasonable measures appropriate to the volume and nature of the data: access restricted to authorized accounts, secrets kept out of public code, field validation and limits, HTTPS transmission and providers with security and confidentiality commitments.

No system connected to the internet can guarantee absolute security. If we identify a relevant incident, we will assess its scope, take containment measures and make the communications required by applicable regulations.

12

Minors, changes and legislation

Minors

Acelera's site and services are aimed at professional contacts, rather than minors. If we discover that we received a minor's information without valid authorization, we will delete it where appropriate.

Changes

We may update this policy when the site, providers or regulations change. The new version will be published here with its effective date. Changes do not permit incompatible uses of previously obtained data without the necessary legal basis or consent.

Legislation and complaints

This policy is interpreted under the laws of the Argentine Republic, without limiting non-waivable rights under other applicable regulations. In addition to Acelera's channel, you may always contact the AAIP or competent courts.

Contact

For any question about this policy or the use of your data, write to contacto@acelera.agency.

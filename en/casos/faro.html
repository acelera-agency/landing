The problem

The data exists, but verifying a lead takes too much work

Information on public works, contracts, agencies and suppliers is spread across portals, files and incompatible formats. Finding a record is not enough: its origin must be preserved, its limits understood and the process reproducible.

  • The same investigation requires moving between portals, CSV files, APIs and documents.
  • Identifiers, names and amounts do not always use the same format.
  • A contract or award can be confused with a payment if context is not preserved.
  • Many records do not have sufficiently precise official geometry to display on a map.

Product goal

Reduce the distance between a lead and its verification.

Faro does not replace investigation. It organizes the process so every claim leads back to a source and shows its limits.

What we built

A complete workflow from search to exportable evidence

The product combines exploration, data normalization and traceability. The map guides users where valid geometry exists; Explorer also keeps records accessible when they cannot be plotted reliably.

Explorer

Search by agency, supplier, source, signal or free text to find a lead without relying on the map.

Records

Each case brings together a summary, key data, sources, limitations and next verification steps.

Receipts

Hashes, paths, identifiers and parser versions preserve a reproducible technical trail.

Evidence packs

The record can be exported with a claims matrix, caveats and an investigative checklist.

A central decision

Prioritize verifiability over a striking conclusion

In a sensitive domain, the interface also shapes which claims seem valid. Faro avoids corruption or guilt scores and distinguishes what a source supports, what is partial and what cannot yet be claimed.

Product rule

Faro does not accuse. It shows where to look.

Traceability is not separate technical documentation: it is part of the product the user sees and exports.

  • A visible official source for each integrated record.
  • Caveats about what that source cannot establish.
  • Invalid geometry preserved as a gap, rather than plotted as certainty.
  • A claims matrix with supported, partial and not supported states.

An explicit limit

This is not a client project.

It demonstrates product, data and engineering capabilities. On its own, it does not demonstrate business results, savings or impact within a company.

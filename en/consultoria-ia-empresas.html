Before choosing a tool

Separate real opportunities from appealing ideas

Consulting turns a broad intention —“we want to use AI”— into decisions about tasks, data, risks and outcomes. It also identifies cases where conventional automation is enough.

  • There are many AI ideas, but no shared criteria for prioritizing them.
  • A team does repetitive work with scattered information and does not know which part to automate.
  • A pilot already exists, but its limits, quality or path to production are unclear.
  • The company needs to evaluate data, security, permissions and integration before committing to an investment.
  • Product or technology teams need an executable scope to work with leadership and operational departments.

Practical judgment

The question is not “where do we put AI?”.

It is which outcome should improve, why AI would be appropriate and how to verify it without shifting hidden risk to users or teams.

Concrete work

From an opportunity map to an implementable plan

The consulting scope adapts to the starting point. It can resolve a specific decision or prepare and build the first use case.

Process map

Users, tasks, information, tools, delays, errors and decisions that currently support the outcome.

Use case prioritization

Comparison by impact, feasibility, data quality, frequency, cost of error and adoption capacity.

Technical assessment

Data sources, permissions, integrations, models, architecture and constraints needed to operate.

Evaluation design

Representative cases, acceptance criteria and limits for deciding whether the outcome is good enough.

Implementation plan

Initial scope, dependencies, owners, risks and a sequence for learning before expanding the investment.

Build

If the case makes sense, we can implement the pilot, integrate it and support it through to production.

How we work

A sequence for deciding with evidence

Not all consulting engagements need to end in development. The goal is a clear decision and preparation for the right next step.

01

Understand

We assess the desired outcome, current process, users and business constraints.

02

Prioritize

We compare opportunities by value, feasibility, risk and actual adoption capacity.

03

Test

When technical evidence is needed, we build a limited test and evaluate it with real cases.

04

Decide

We deliver a recommendation, the next scope and the conditions for moving forward or stopping.

A clear conclusion

What you receive at the end

The company should be able to explain what it decided, with what evidence and what it needs to execute the next step.

  • Defined problem, users and expected outcome.
  • Prioritized use cases and justified exclusions.
  • Risks, data, integrations and evaluation criteria.
  • An actionable recommendation and implementation plan.

Frequently asked questions

What to expect from applied AI consulting

Does Acelera only advise, or also implement?

We can do both. Consulting defines what is worth doing and why; when the case is feasible, the same team can design, develop, integrate and deploy the solution.

Does the company need a data or AI team?

Not necessarily. We do need access to people who understand the process and relevant information sources. We also identify which internal or external capabilities will be needed to operate the solution.

How do you choose the first use case?

We look for a frequent task with a verifiable outcome, accessible data and a manageable cost of error. We prioritize learning from a useful scope before attempting broad transformation.

Can you work with the internal product or technology team?

Yes. We can contribute discovery, scope definition, design, architecture, implementation or review depending on what the team already covers. We document decisions and avoid creating unnecessary dependency.

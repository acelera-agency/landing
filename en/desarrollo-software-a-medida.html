Decisions before code

When custom development makes sense

Custom software is useful when an important company process does not fit well into a standard tool, or when connecting what already exists creates a concrete advantage.

  • Operations depend on spreadsheets, messages and duplicated manual tasks.
  • A standard system forces the team to work around its limitations.
  • Data, permissions or actions need to be integrated across tools that are currently isolated.
  • Customers or internal teams need a portal, workflow or application with their own rules.
  • The digital product needs a specific capability that the current team cannot tackle alone.

We also say no

Not everything needs new software.

If configuration, simple automation or an existing tool solves the problem with less risk, that is the right recommendation.

Frequently asked questions

What matters before we start

Is Acelera an agency, a consultancy or a software development company?

Acelera is a boutique software development company and product consultancy: we help define what is worth building and can design, develop and take it into production. Our approach is for companies that need a specific solution, rather than fixed packages.

How long does custom development take?

It depends on the scope and integrations. The initial stage usually takes around a week, and a usable first version can take between two and six weeks. We make assumptions and risks explicit before we start.

Who owns the code and access?

Our default approach is for the company to retain control of the code, repositories, deployment and accounts needed to operate. The details are explicitly agreed for each project.

What technologies do you work with?

We choose the architecture based on the product, the team that will maintain it, integrations and operating costs. We do not force a technology when another option reduces risk or makes continuity easier.

Do you develop AI applications?

Yes. Acelera designs and develops web applications, internal platforms and digital products that incorporate AI when it solves a specific need. Depending on the case, the solution may include agents, automation, integrations or features using your own data, with permissions, evaluation and human review before expanding its use.
